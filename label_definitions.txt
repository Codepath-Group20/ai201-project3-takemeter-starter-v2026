nuanced_critique: A thoughtful, balanced review that weighs both positive and negative aspects of a film while grounding its critique in specific technical elements (e.g., acting, pacing, or cinematography).
blind_hype_or_rant: An extreme emotional review featuring pure gushing praise or furious hatred without structural balance or deep technical analysis.
factual_summary_recap: A review that focuses primarily on summarizing plot progression, setting, or narrative events without offering subjective value judgments.
